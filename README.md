I like building AI systems people can actually trust: LLM agents that check their answers against real sources, and PyTorch models I can explain, not just train. I started in Computer Engineering, then did a Master's in Applied and Computational Mathematics at Simon Fraser University. I also have a strong data background, from Kafka and Spark pipelines to Power BI dashboards.

Based in Toronto. Open to AI/ML Engineer and Data Scientist roles anywhere in Canada.

### Right now

- Building a research assistant for molecular biology questions in [Dr. Cedric Chauve's lab](https://cchauve.github.io) at SFU. It pulls papers from PubMed and NCBI (RAG), drafts an answer, and runs an evidence check before citing anything. Built with LangGraph and FastAPI.
- Working on [Toronto Bike Share](https://github.com/sadeghianmr/toronto-bikeshare-analytics): do new bike lanes actually bring more riders to nearby stations? R, PostgreSQL/PostGIS, and a difference-in-differences study.

### Selected projects

**AI and machine learning**

|---|---|
| [SkyRoom](https://github.com/Daniel-c137/stormhacks-2026-MoE) · [Devpost](https://devpost.com/software/skyroom) | AI meeting assistant built at StormHacks 2026. Ask it questions out loud mid-meeting; it answers from past meetings, GitHub, and Jira. |
